About Me

I'm a Psychology + Disruptive Tech graduate with a curiosity for human behavior, user experience, and new technology. This page is where I lay out the principles that shape how I approach problems.

I see the whole picture.

I don't just look at the problem — I look at what led to it. Years of coding taught me that the bug crashing your site might trace back to the very first line you wrote.

I let go of unnecessary stress.

I don't buy into "stress = productivity." A little pressure can sharpen focus, but I've learned that over-stressing does more harm than good — and I trust that things tend to work out.

I treat failure as data.

Every failed experiment tells me something the successful ones can't — it's not a setback, it's information I didn't have before.
