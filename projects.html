Projects & CV

A few pieces of work that reflect how I think and what I care about — psychology, behaviour change, and where technology fits into both.

SleepSync — A Digital Intervention for Sleep Behaviour in Young Adults

Most sleep interventions rely on education or strict schedules — and both tend to fail. I designed SleepSync, a digital intervention concept grounded in the COM-B model of behaviour change, that instead targets why young adults skip sleep in the first place: bedtime procrastination, late-night phone use, and the belief that sleep is "unproductive." Rather than tracking sleep scores (which research shows can backfire into anxiety-driven "orthosomnia"), SleepSync links behaviour directly to next-day cognitive performance, reframing sleep as something that pays off immediately, not just eventually.

Social Robots as Behaviour-Change Agents — Autonomy and Legal Accountability

This case study looks at what happens when a humanoid robot successfully changes someone's behaviour — and who's responsible if it goes wrong. Using a real pilot study where a social robot reduced high-calorie snacking by over 50%, I examined the ethical tension between effective behavioural influence and genuine human autonomy, and traced how current EU AI and liability law fails to clearly assign responsibility when harm emerges gradually through influence rather than a single failure. I proposed concrete design and regulatory fixes — including built-in reflective prompts and binding, not voluntary, accountability rules.

Empathy for Animals — Predicting Prosocial Behaviour, Personality, and Narcissism

My dissertation asked a simple question with a surprisingly clear answer: does how someone treats animals predict how they treat people? Surveying 112 young adults, I found that empathy toward animals significantly predicted higher prosocial behaviour and agreeableness, and lower narcissism — suggesting concern for animals isn't a separate trait, but part of a broader empathic orientation. It was my first time running a full quantitative study end to end: designing the survey, collecting and cleaning real data, and running the regression analyses myself in SPSS.
